# Home Insurance Policy: Coverage Terms

## Storm and Weather Damage
Damage caused by storms, falling trees, hail, or wind is covered up to the
full dwelling coverage limit stated on the policy declarations page.

## Filing Deadline
Claims must be filed within 60 days of the incident date. Claims filed later
than 60 days require an exception review before approval.

## Exclusions
The following are not covered under standard home policies:
- Flood damage (requires separate flood insurance)
- Damage from lack of maintenance or gradual deterioration
- Damage occurring while the home was vacant for more than 30 consecutive
  days without prior notice to the insurer

## Maximum Payout
The maximum payout for any single home claim under a standard policy is
$50,000, unless the policy has an extended dwelling coverage rider.

## Deductible
Standard deductible is $1,000 per claim, deducted from the payout amount.
