# Auto Insurance Policy: Coverage Terms

## Glass and Windshield Damage
Windshield and glass damage claims are covered up to $2,000 per incident
without requiring senior adjuster approval. Claims above $2,000 for glass
damage alone require manual review before payout.

## Filing Deadline
Claims must be filed within 30 days of the incident date. Claims filed later
than 30 days are not eligible for automatic approval and require an
exception review.

## Exclusions
The following are not covered under standard auto policies:
- Damage occurring during a race, rally, or speed contest
- Damage while the vehicle was being used for commercial ride-sharing or
  delivery services, unless a commercial rider is attached to the policy
- Wear and tear, mechanical breakdown, or pre-existing damage

## Maximum Payout
The maximum payout for any single auto claim under a standard policy is
$25,000, unless the policy has a comprehensive collision rider.

## Deductible
Standard deductible is $500 per claim, deducted from the payout amount.
